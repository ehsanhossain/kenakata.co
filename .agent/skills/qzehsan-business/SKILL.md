## Architecture philosophy (non-negotiable)

- **No frontend-to-LLM direct calls** in serious products — backend orchestration always
- **Laravel** preferred backend
- **Rule-based / deterministic filtering BEFORE AI enrichment**
- AI used for extraction, reranking, explanation — not raw decision-making
- Store evidence, confidence scores, prompts, model metadata — auditability matters
- Production-grade, not flashy demos
