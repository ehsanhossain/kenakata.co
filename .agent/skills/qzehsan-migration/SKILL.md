# qzehsan-migration

## The strategic goal

**Migrate to Australia or Canada → first-world residency → first-world passport.**

Stated plainly: he does not want to remain a Bangladeshi passport holder long-term. The visa friction he's already faced (Indonesia refusal), the financial friction of conventional travel, and the limited mobility all reinforce this.

## The primary path: Australia Master's by Research

**Why Master's by Research (as opposed to Coursework):**
- Often cheaper overall (scholarship-eligible, research stipends)
- Heavier GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) weight — harder to get but stronger intent signal
- Pipeline into PhD and academic residency routes
- Post-study work visa (subclass 485) available on completion
- Stronger pathway to skilled independent (subclass 189) or skilled nominated (subclass 190) PR later

## The sponsor-timing risk (critical)

Father's L4–L5 decompression surgery is a near-term reality. **Document signing and financial statements require father to be physically and mentally available.** If surgery + recovery coincides with visa application window:
- Pre-op: 2–4 week prep period (medical clearances)
- Surgery: 1–2 day hospital stay (endoscopic)
- Post-op recovery: 4–8 weeks for daily function; 3–6 months for full recovery
- Compound cognitive effects from anesthesia + pain meds may briefly affect document execution

**Mitigation strategies:**
1. Gather + sign all sponsor documents **before** surgery
2. Establish co-sponsor as backup (mother? sisters? Ehsan himself using his own financials?)
3. Apply for visa in a window that gives father 2–3 months of post-op stabilization before the application peak
4. If surgery is delayed — re-sequence accordingly

## Alternative paths (worth keeping in mind)

### Canada
- **Express Entry (CRS-based):** requires strong IELTS (CLB 9+ ideal = IELTS ~7.5), work experience, education credentials
- **Study permit + PGWP pathway:** Master's + 3-year PGWP + PR via Express Entry or PNP
- **Canada tends to be less sponsor-evidence-heavy** than Australia student visa — worth investigating if father's sponsor capacity is a concern

### Australia — other pathways (backup)
- **Subclass 482 (TSS):** employer-sponsored skilled work — if TCG/TVC have or could establish AU presence
- **Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent):** points-based; requires skilled occupation on MLTSSL; age 25–32 = 30 pts
- **Subclass 491:** regional skilled — lower points threshold, 5-year PR pathway

### Others (lower priority but worth noting)
- **UK:** Graduate route (2 years post-study work); limited PR pathway without specific route
- **New Zealand:** Skilled Migrant Category; less competitive than AU
- **Germany:** Study + EU Blue Card (tech/consulting roles feasible); path to citizenship after 5–8 years

3. Be factual, not florid
